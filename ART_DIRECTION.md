# Bear Kart art direction

Warm cream typography, pine-green surfaces, burnt-orange accents, restrained racing-club graphics. Bear's actual tall ears, cinnamon coat, cream chest, and hazel eyes are the identity reference. No original photographs were replaced.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, not a CLI or external API script. Both selected images are copied into the project and referenced by the game.

## assets/bear-kart-hero.png

Reference: `assets/bear_front_small.png`. Used as garage background art; not represented as live gameplay.

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic wide hero artwork for a premium browser kart racing game named Bear Kart. Reference image: identity reference of the user's actual puppy Bear. Preserve his cinnamon brown fluffy fur, cream chest, tall triangular ears, hazel green eyes, dark brown muzzle and happy expression. Render Bear as a high quality stylized 3D game character driving a beautifully detailed small burnt-orange racing kart with cream racing stripe, black rubber racing tires, metal suspension, number 01 on nose. No helmet obscuring ears. Scene: sweeping alpine forest racetrack at golden hour, red cream kerbs, pine trees, distant hazy mountains, warm sunlight and subtle dust, premium polished game key art, convincing materials and cinematic depth, not childish clipart. Composition: landscape 1536x1024 or wider. Kart and Bear occupy right two-thirds with full kart visible, front three-quarter angle pointing toward lower left. Left third is dark forest and atmospheric negative space for HTML heading overlay. No typography, no logos, no watermark, no UI. Color palette warm orange, deep pine green, warm cream.
